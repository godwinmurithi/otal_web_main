Course Overview

Start your GIS journey with confidence

GIS is one of the most valuable digital skills today — supporting land administration, planning, utilities, environmental management, infrastructure, research, and development across every sector worldwide.

Gentle Introduction to GIS in QGIS Software is designed to make GIS simple, practical, and accessible. The course gives you a clear entry point into GIS by focusing on essential concepts, real workflows, and hands-on experience in QGIS.

Instead of overwhelming you with theory, the course walks you through practical exercises that build confidence as you learn. You will explore how geographic data is organised, how different data types are used, and how to transform raw information into meaningful spatial insights.

Curriculum

A step-by-step path from GIS basics to practical QGIS use

01

Introduction to GIS

Understand what GIS is, how it works, and where it is applied across sectors and industries around the world.

02

Getting Started with QGIS

Learn the QGIS interface, tools, and essential workflows — everything you need to navigate the software confidently.

03

Understanding Spatial Data

Explore vector, raster, and tabular data and how they relate and work together in GIS projects.

04

Importing and Preparing Data

Bring datasets into QGIS and prepare them for mapping and analysis, including coordinate systems.

05

Basic Spatial Analysis

Carry out joins, overlays, and introductory vector and raster analysis tasks with real data.

06

Buffer and Distance Analysis

Use distance-based methods to answer practical spatial questions relevant to infrastructure, land, and services.

07

Working with Elevation and Land Cover

Understand how terrain and land cover data support analysis and decision-making in real projects.

08

Map Design and Presentation

Create simple, clear, and professional maps for communication, reporting, and stakeholder presentation.
